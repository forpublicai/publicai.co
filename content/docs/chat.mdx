**Public AI Chat** ([`chat.publicai.co`](https://chat.publicai.co)) is the public web interface for interacting with sovereign and open-source AI models hosted on the Public AI Inference Utility.

## Ways to Access Public AI Chat

1. **Full Web App ([`chat.publicai.co`](https://chat.publicai.co)):** Sign in for persistent chat history, multi-model comparison (`Apertus 1.5 70B`, `SEA-LION v4`, `Bielik`, `ALIA`, `OLMo 3`), document uploads, web search, and national RAG knowledgebases.
2. **Instant Guest Chat ([`/chat`](/chat)):** Try `Apertus 1.5` right on `publicai.co/chat` with zero login required.
3. **Public AI Mobile App ([`/products/chat#mobile`](/products/chat#mobile) — *Coming Soon*):** Native iOS and Android experience with voice mode and camera document scanning.

## Key Features

- **Community-Governed National System Prompts:** Inspect and contribute to our open-source system prompts on GitHub ([Switzerland](https://github.com/forpublicai/chat.publicai.co/blob/main/community/system_prompts/switzerland.md), [Singapore](https://github.com/forpublicai/chat.publicai.co/blob/main/community/system_prompts/singapore.md)).
- **National Knowledgebases (`#` command):** Type `#` in the chat input while logged in at `chat.publicai.co` to reference curated national and civic RAG collections.
- **Multimodal Vision Input:** Upload images, charts, or scanned PDFs when chatting with vision-enabled models like `Apertus 1.5`.
- **Privacy by Default:** Your conversations are never sold or used for model training unless you explicitly opt into a public data-flywheel program (see our [Privacy & Data Flywheel Policy](/tc#23-special-program-data-flywheel-opt-in)).
