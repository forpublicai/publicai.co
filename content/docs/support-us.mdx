Support our mission to make AI accessible to everyone. Contribute any amount you're comfortable with — every dollar makes a difference, starting from just \$1.

## What You Get

Contributing via OpenCollective upgrades your account to the **Plus** tier:

- **Higher rate limits**: `200 requests/minute` (up from `100` on the Free tier)
- **Community impact**: Direct contribution to democratizing AI technology and funding the free public tier for everyone

For **Pro** (`300 req/min`) or **Enterprise** (`10,000 req/min`) tiers, contact [support@publicai.co](mailto:support@publicai.co).

See [Plans & Rate Limits](/docs/plans) for the full tier breakdown.

**[Contribute on OpenCollective ↗](https://opencollective.com/publicai/contribute/i-public-ai-93476/checkout?interval=month&amount=5&contributeAs=me)**

## Important Notes

To receive the Plus tier upgrade, you need to:

1. Have issued at least one API key in the [developer portal](https://platform.publicai.co/settings/api-keys)
2. Use the **same email address** in your OpenCollective account

If you experience any issues with your contribution or rate limit upgrades, please drop us a note at [support@publicai.co](mailto:support@publicai.co).
