*The Public AI Inference Utility is now an [official inference provider](https://huggingface.co/publicai) on Hugging Face, bringing public AI to developers worldwide.*

<img src="/people/julien_chaumond.jpg" alt="Julien Chaumond" />

Through this collaboration, users can now access and test public and sovereign AI models like Switzerland's [Apertus](https://huggingface.co/swiss-ai/Apertus-8B-Instruct-2509?inference_provider=publicai) and Singapore's [SEA-LION](https://huggingface.co/aisingapore/Gemma-SEA-LION-v4-27B-IT?inference_provider=publicai) directly through the developer-friendly Hugging Face ecosystem. This is an important step for making public AI more accessible to developers, researchers, and organizations worldwide.

- [Try our models on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/models?inference_provider=publicai&sort=trending)
- [See our organization card](https://huggingface.co/publicai) on Hugging Face
- [Learn more about our developer platform](/docs)

🙏 to [Julien](https://www.linkedin.com/in/julienchaumond/) (pictured above) and [Simon](https://www.linkedin.com/in/simon-brandeis/) (pictured below) from HF, who were super helpful & responsive in setting this up! The entire process was surprisingly easy; we just made a few PRs directly to the HF site. Also hat tip to [Poli](https://www.linkedin.com/in/apolinariosteps/) (not pictured) who made the connection.

## What this means for developers

As an official Hugging Face inference provider, Public AI now offers:

- **Direct API access** to public and sovereign models through Hugging Face's infrastructure
- **Seamless integration** with existing Hugging Face workflows and tools
- **Global availability** with our distributed inference network
- **Transparent pricing** and usage tracking through Hugging Face's platform

This partnership makes it easier than ever for developers to build applications using public AI models, whether you're prototyping a new idea or deploying at scale.

## Getting started

Ready to try public AI on Hugging Face? Here's how to get started:

- **Browse our models**: Visit our [Hugging Face organization](https://huggingface.co/publicai) to see available models
- **API access**: Use Hugging Face's inference API with our models
- **Documentation**: Check out our [developer docs](/docs) for detailed integration guides

This partnership is just the beginning. We're committed to expanding our model offerings on Hugging Face, including future releases from our partner institutions at national labs and national champions.
